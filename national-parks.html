Mount Rainier National Park

An ice-covered volcano rising above ancient forests and summer wildflower meadows.

Photos coming soon
About the park
  • The summit reaches 14,410 feet above sea level.
  • The park protects 236,381 acres.
  • About 97% of the park is designated wilderness.
  • Glaciers supply five major river systems.
  • The 93-mile Wonderland Trail circles the mountain.
Source: National Park Service

North Cascades National Park

Jagged peaks, deep forested valleys, and an extraordinary concentration of glaciers.

Photos coming soon
About the park
  • The park complex contains more than 300 glaciers.
  • More than 300 lakes and ponds dot the park complex.
  • Goode Mountain rises to 9,206 feet.
  • Eight distinct life zones range from lowland forests to alpine peaks.
  • The complex includes the national park, Ross Lake, and Lake Chelan recreation areas.
Source: National Park Service
