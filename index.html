Olympic National Park

Nearly a million acres of glacier-capped mountains, moss-covered rain forests, and wild Pacific coastline. Olympic’s size and variety make each part of the park feel like a different world.

  • 922,651Acres to exploreNearly a million acres on the Olympic Peninsula.
  • 3Distinct ecosystemsGlacier-capped mountains, rain forests, and Pacific coast.
  • 73Miles of wild coastSea stacks, beaches, and tide pools.
  • 95%Designated wildernessProtected, undeveloped landscapes.
  • 1,100+Plant speciesFrom forest floors to alpine meadows.
Source: National Park Service
